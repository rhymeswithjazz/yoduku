How to play

A hidden path visits every open cell once, numbered 1 to the end. Consecutive numbers always sit in touching cells — no diagonals.

  • Tap a cell next to your chain's glowing head to place the next number.
  • Gold numbers are yours. Etched numbers are fixed clues your path must reach in order.
  • Tap one of your numbers to clear it and everything after it.
  • ◉ cells are a wormhole — they count as touching.
  • Dark cells are void. The path goes around them.

Fill the board to complete the constellation. "Still on track?" tells you whether your chain can still be completed — each use costs bragging rights.
